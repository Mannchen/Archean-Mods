[h2] Documentation [/h2]
[h3] CheckValve [/h3]

[u] Description [/u]
The Check Valve will only let fluid pass in one direction, indicated by the arrow on the side.

[h3] Chiller [/h3]

[u] Description [/u]
The Chiller cools fluids pumped through the fluid ports on the side down to a selected temperature.

[u] Usage [/u]
Pump fluid through the fluid ports on the side. The Chiller will cool the fluid to the temperature selected via the 
screen or data input.  
The Chiller needs a data signal on channel 0 and power to operate.  
The heat removed from the fluid and waste heat is transfered into the component and must be removed by pumping fluid 
through the fluid ports in the back.
Fluid can only be cooled to 300K below the Chiller's core temperature.  
The Chiller can consume up to 2MW of power.

[code]The chiller has no internal fluid buffer. Make sure the fluid has somewhere to go or no fluid will flow 
(pumping a full tank into itself will not work) [/code]

[h3] HeatExchanger [/h3]

[u] Description [/u]
The HeatExchanger transfers heat between fluid pumped through the top, bottom (shell) and front, back (tube).
The HeatExchanger has an internal heat buffer and will store some heat.

[h3] InlineTank [/h3]

[u] Description [/u]
A small Tank with two fluid ports.
Capacity: [i]0.02 m³[/i]

[h3] PipeSensor [/h3]

[u] Description [/u]
PipeSensor is a component that outputs information about fluid flow, temperature and composition through a pipe.
Flow is measured directionally, and flow in both directions will cancel each other out.
Composition is mass not percentage.
